Questions

Free Focus Timer & To-Do List: FAQ

Is LISSO a free online focus timer?

Yes. LISSO is completely free to use in your browser. There is no account, no sign-up and no subscription. Open the workspace, start the focus timer and begin working.

Can I use LISSO as a Pomodoro timer?

LISSO has a countdown focus timer that starts at 25 minutes, the classic Pomodoro length, and lets you add 1, 5, 15 or 25 minutes as you go. Use it for Pomodoro-style focus sprints and take your own breaks between them.

Does LISSO have brown noise, rain sounds and binaural beats?

Yes. The Focus Room generates rain, brown noise, pink noise and binaural beats directly in your browser, so there is nothing to download. Many people use brown noise for studying, reading and deep work.

Is there a free to-do list with priorities and no sign-up?

Yes. Add tasks to your to-do list, give each one a High, Medium or Low priority and a deadline, and LISSO sorts them for you. You can also group tasks into projects with milestones and see them on a monthly calendar.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your tasks, notes and streaks are saved locally in your browser, not on a server, and LISSO has no tracking and no AI assistant reading your work.

Who is LISSO for?

Students who need a study timer and planner, freelancers who want a simple task manager, and small teams who want quick meeting rooms without installing anything.
